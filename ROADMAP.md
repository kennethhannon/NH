# PracticalKit revenue roadmap

Target: small, high-quality utilities with narrow search intent and low hosting costs. Public beta does not yet collect payments.

## Candidate niches

| App | Primary buyer | Proposed paid offer (hypothesis) | Difficulty to sell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Settlement Studio | total-loss car claimants | $9-19 structured report w/ comps | one-time demand; acquisition hard |
| FinishLine | DIY renovators, inspectors, owners | $19-39 per project report / $12 monthly for contractors | stronger B2B potential |
| Shift Math | nurses, medical staff, overnight workers | free checker + $5-10 monthly for history and shifts | recurring but needs payroll-grade precision |
| Shift Roster | 12h shift workers | $3-6/month schedules and premium forecast | crowded but niche |
| Renovation Scope | small remodelers | $29/month estimate & scope templates | more likely B2B demand |
| Used Car Risk Lens | budget used-car buyers | $9 research report, not guaranteed reliability | high acquisition challenge |
| EV Total-Cost Lab | EV shoppers | $5 scenario export | crowded; strong SEO angle |
| Macro Pressure | retail investors | $3 or $10 monthly macro dashboard | needs licensed, current data |
| HVAC Job Quote | solo HVAC shops | $29-59/month work orders | verify licensing/workflow needs |
| Specialty Lab QC | lab professionals | site license for non-patient QC scheduling | medical compliance/accuracy burden |

## Stage gates

1. Discovery: 10 customer conversations and a landing page with signups (no fake counters).
2. Prototype: solve a concrete task and verify calculation edge cases.
3. Quality: accessibility, cross-device browser tests, written privacy/terms, backups, support address.
4. Monetize: live payment processor only after the user explicitly provides/authorizes their account.
5. Scale only apps with traffic, repeat use, or first paying customers.

Warning: pricing and revenue here are hypotheses. No customers, revenue, or profitability is implied.
