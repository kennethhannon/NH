Privacy & beta terms

Our calculators and project tools run on your device. You decide when to export or share a report.

Information you enter

PracticalKit's current browser applications perform calculations locally. They do not require accounts or submit form entries to an application server. Some use your browser's local storage to retain entries. The information stays on that browser profile unless you manually export it, share a file, clear local storage, or another application with access to your device reads it.

What hosting can see

The web host or your internet provider may process ordinary network metadata such as your IP address and pages requested. No advertising or analytics scripts are loaded at present. If ads are activated, our policy will be updated to disclose the ad provider, cookies, user controls, and consent where required. Hosting-provider logging and security processing may still apply.

Your controls

Use Reset or Clear within an app to remove its local saved project data. You may also clear website data in browser settings. Backups saved to your device remain your responsibility. Browser storage is not permanent, synced, or guaranteed: export a backup for important projects.

Financial, labor, and contractor disclaimers

All reports are estimates for personal planning or recordkeeping. They are not legal, accounting, tax, insurance, wage-law, inspection, or professional construction advice. Actual obligations depend on law, contract terms, policy, and your circumstances. Verify calculations, taxes, and requirements independently before sending a quote or relying on a settlement or wage estimate.

Payments and paid accounts

No checkout, subscription, paid account, or paid service is currently offered through this beta. If paid features launch, their price, processing, and refund terms will be shown before purchase.

Contact and feedback

While this beta is under review, you can open a public issue at the project issue tracker. Do not include confidential customer, payroll, or insurance details in a public issue. A private support address will be published before paid offerings begin.
